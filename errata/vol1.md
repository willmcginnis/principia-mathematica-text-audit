# Volume I (Gutenberg #78050): draft errata report, checked by a person, not yet sent

**Status: the human check is complete; nothing on this page has been sent to Project Gutenberg yet.** This page
lists all 102 entries of the earlier draft (80 in the draft report, 22 held back) and what the human check found
for each. On 2026-10-11 one person checked every entry against the scan, blind: they were never told which
version was the ebook's and which the proposed correction, and no machine pick or score was shown. For 49 of
the 113 items checked (the 102 entries and the 11 additions below) they saw a crop of the page around the line
and the two versions of the disputed span in random order, and picked the one that matches the print. For the
other 64, where the two versions differ in a single character, they saw the printed line with that spot boxed
by our glyph matcher and tapped what is printed there, from 10 tiles of glyphs cut from PM's own pages; both
versions' glyphs were always among the tiles, which were in a fixed grouped order, never ranked, and the tap
was mapped to the ebook's version, the correction or neither afterwards (see the
[README](../README.md#human-review)). The result:

- **96 entries** are in the revised draft report (section 2): the **76** of the earlier draft report that the
  check confirmed, and **20** of the 22 held-back entries, which the check confirmed and which were moved in.
- **1** is removed: the reviewer picked the ebook's text, that is, the reviewer found that the print (in the one
  scan) agrees with the ebook (draft entry 20, ✱25·37).
- **5** are held: the reviewer could not confirm them. They are not in the revised draft report.

The revised report is a draft. Will McGinnis will send it; it has not been sent. The same check also covered
the 11 draft additions on this page (✱4·42 in section 4 and the 10 candidates of section 5): all 11 were
confirmed. They are not yet merged into the report.

What the check does and does not establish: one reviewer, and one scan. The check compared each entry with the
same scan the models used. The University of Michigan copy we tried as a second witness turned out to be the
same scan ([METHODOLOGY §7](../METHODOLOGY.md#7-limitations)), so where a mark is faint or broken, whether the
defect is in the print or in the scan is open. The report's own introduction below says how each entry was
checked, and [METHODOLOGY.md](../METHODOLOGY.md) has the details. These entries are separate from the human
precision sample (see the [README](../README.md#human-review)).

Further Vol I candidates from the full-page pass (Introduction and body) are not on this page. Only a sample of
them was checked by a person (see the [README](../README.md#human-review)); the others are awaiting human check.
Section 4 has ✱4·42, a body row that the Lean replay found again independently. It is one of the 215 body rows,
not an additional candidate, and it is not one of the 102 entries. Section 5 has 10 new candidates from the
follow-up of the body pages that had no second reading: 8 from 2026-10-09 and 2 more from 2026-10-10. They are
not among the 102 entries either.
