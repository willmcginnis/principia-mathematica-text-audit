# Contributing

Thank you for helping. The digital texts checked here are careful work by Project Gutenberg and the
Distributed Proofreaders (DP) volunteers; every report is offered as a contribution to that work, not as a
criticism of it.

## Reporting a discrepancy

If you think a row here is wrong, or you have found a discrepancy between a digital text and the printed book,
please open an issue. It helps if the issue gives:

- the volume and the text: Gutenberg #78050 (Vol I) or #78255 (Vol II), with the ebook version or date if
  you know it;
- the printed page, and the proposition number (✱N·M) if there is one;
- where the page is in the scan you used (for example the scan image number; see
  [METHODOLOGY §1](METHODOLOGY.md#1-sources) for the scans and their page offsets);
- what the digital text has and what the print has, with a few words of context;
- if you are disputing or confirming a row listed here, its entry or row number in
  [errata/vol1.md](errata/vol1.md) or [errata/vol2.md](errata/vol2.md).

A crop or screenshot of the printed line is welcome. Please judge against the scan, not against what the
mathematics should say: PM's own slips are part of the print.

## How entries are checked

Model output is treated as evidence, not as truth. Every errata entry is checked against the scan by a person
before it is submitted upstream, and anything that no person has checked yet is labelled "awaiting human
check". A report from an issue goes through the same check before it is added to a list or sent anywhere.
Will McGinnis sends the reports, so please do not send reports to Project Gutenberg or DP on this project's
behalf.

## No unposted Distributed Proofreaders text

DP's Volume III text is unpublished work in progress, so this repository gives counts only and does not quote
or redistribute it. Please do the same: do not paste DP's unposted text into issues or pull requests. For
Vol III, the printed page and a description of the difference are enough.
